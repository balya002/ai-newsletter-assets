A quick industry snapshot on how AI is changing advertising across planning, measurement, creative, and commerce.
✨ Search planning is moving deeper into AI-led intent discovery
Google announced that Dynamic Search Ads will be upgraded to AI Max for Search campaigns, with legacy DSA features beginning to move into AI Max starting in September.
Why it matters: search planning is becoming less centered on manually defined keyword coverage and more centered on AI interpreting intent, landing pages, creative assets, and conversion signals.
What this means for advertiser conversations: clients may care less about whether AI is being used and more about what controls, reporting, landing page logic, and success signals are in place.
Read Google Update
✨ AI measurement is becoming a bigger buy-side priority
IAB’s State of Data 2026 report focuses on how AI is reshaping measurement across attribution, incrementality, and marketing mix modeling.
Why it matters: as measurement gets more fragmented, AI is being positioned as a way to connect signals, identify patterns, and make performance readouts more actionable.
What this means for advertiser conversations: reporting discussions should connect delivery updates to learning agendas, test design, incrementality questions, and the data inputs needed to make AI-supported measurement useful.
Read IAB Report
✨ AI creative tools are speeding up production, but QA matters more
Meta’s growing AI creative toolset is getting more attention from advertisers, but marketers are also flagging concerns around unwanted AI-generated edits, asset distortion, and control over how creative is modified.
Why it matters: AI can make creative versioning faster, but it also creates a stronger need for brand guidelines, review checkpoints, and clarity around what should or should not be automated.
What this means for advertiser conversations: creative AI should be framed around speed and testing support, not replacing judgment. The strongest use cases still need human review and clear approval guardrails.
Read Coverage
✨ Commerce media is becoming more AI-assisted
Amazon highlighted continued Amazon Ads growth alongside AI-powered tools and expanded reach across shopping and streaming environments.
Why it matters: shopping journeys are becoming more conversational and recommendation-led, which can change how advertisers think about discovery, product context, and lower-funnel influence.
What this means for advertiser conversations: AI is not only a workflow tool. It is also becoming part of the consumer path to discovery, consideration, and purchase.
Read Amazon Update

📌 Template: “Clean Up My Rough Notes”

Use case: Turning messy bullets, Slack/Teams notes, or rough thoughts into a clear internal update

Clean up the rough notes below into a clear internal update.

Keep it concise and easy to skim.
Organize it into:
• What happened
• What needs attention
• Next steps

Use a natural, professional tone. Do not add information that is not in my notes.

Notes:
[paste rough notes]
✨ Hidden Feature: Compare Multiple Files in Copilot

Why it is useful: Instead of opening several decks, notes, or documents one by one, Copilot can help compare them and pull out what changed, what conflicts, or what needs attention.

Quick example:
Attach or reference two versions of a deck, brief, or recap and ask Copilot to compare them. This works especially well for spotting changed recommendations, missing details, duplicated sections, or new questions that need follow-up.
Tip: Use this for deck revisions, campaign setup notes, client briefs, and longer internal documents where small changes are easy to miss.
