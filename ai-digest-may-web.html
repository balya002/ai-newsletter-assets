AI Industry Snapshot: key shifts across advertising, planning, measurement, and creative — with links to the source articles.
🔎 AI is changing how search campaigns are built
Google is moving Dynamic Search Ads, automatically created assets, and broad match campaigns toward AI Max, with automatic upgrades beginning in September.
  • Planning shift: search setup is becoming more automated and intent-led.
  • Client impact: advertisers will likely ask for more clarity around controls, query matching, and brand safety.
  • What to watch: how much visibility platforms give buyers as AI campaign types expand.
Read Google Update
📊 Measurement is becoming an AI priority
IAB’s State of Data 2026 report focuses on how AI is reshaping attribution, incrementality, and marketing mix modeling.
  • Measurement shift: AI is being used to help connect fragmented signals across platforms and channels.
  • Client impact: advertisers may expect faster insight cycles, but will still need clear methodology and confidence in the data.
  • What to watch: whether AI improves decision-making or simply makes imperfect measurement move faster.
View IAB Report
🎨 AI creative tools are moving into standard ad workflows
Meta’s AI creative push shows how platforms are making creative generation, versioning, and optimization a more built-in part of campaign execution.
  • Creative shift: more variations can be produced faster, especially for testing and platform-specific formats.
  • Client impact: creative volume may increase, but brand voice and quality control still matter.
  • What to watch: where teams draw the line between AI-assisted production and final creative approval.
Read Creative Coverage
🛒 Commerce, streaming, and AI are getting more connected
Amazon Ads reported $17.2B in Q1 advertising revenue, pointing to continued growth across streaming, shopping, and AI-powered ad tools.
  • Planning shift: commerce signals and streaming inventory are becoming more connected in full-funnel buys.
  • Client impact: advertisers may increasingly expect planning to connect awareness, consideration, and purchase signals.
  • What to watch: how platforms package AI, data, and premium video together.
Read Amazon Update

📌 Template: “Clean Up My Rough Notes”

Use case: Turning messy bullets, Teams notes, or rough thoughts into a clear internal update

Clean up the rough notes below into a clear internal update.

Keep it concise and easy to skim.
Organize it into:
• What happened
• What needs attention
• Next steps

Use a natural, professional tone. Do not add information that is not in my notes.

Notes:
[paste rough notes]
✨ Hidden Feature: Compare Multiple Files with Copilot

Use case: Quickly checking differences across decks, docs, recaps, or planning materials

Instead of opening files side by side, you can ask Copilot to compare multiple documents and pull out what changed, what overlaps, or what may need follow-up.
Compare these files and summarize the key differences.

Focus on:
• What changed
• Any conflicting information
• Items that may need follow-up
• The most important takeaway

Keep the summary concise and easy to skim.

Tip: This works best when the files are saved in OneDrive or SharePoint and Copilot has access to them.
